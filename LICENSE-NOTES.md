# Licensing notes

This repository holds image data, not software, and the material in it does not all
come from the same place. The `LICENSE` file at the root states the default terms;
the exceptions below take precedence for the files they cover.

## Default: CC BY 4.0

Everything not listed under "Exceptions" is released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence, matching the terms Virtual Fly
Brain publishes for the corresponding dataset, BrainName neuropils on adult brain
JFRC2: <https://virtualflybrain.org/reports/JenettShinomya_BrainName>

This covers:

- `combinedIndexFiles/`, `individualDomainFiles/` — the BrainName label field
  (mask dated 2013-08-19) and its per-domain files
- `maxProjectionViews/` — projections and thumbnails derived from the above
- `refData/` — index-to-anatomy mappings against FlyBase anatomy ontology IDs
- `bridging/` — CMTK warp fields computed by Virtual Fly Brain

The label field was generated by Arnim Jenett (Janelia Research Campus), Kazunori
Shinomiya and Kei Ito (University of Tokyo) to the nomenclature of Ito et al. 2014,
*Neuron* 81(4):755-765, <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.12.017>. Attribution
should be to that paper.

## Exceptions

### JFRCtemplate2010 (JFRC2) — Janelia / FlyLight

`template/` contains JFRCtemplate2010 and derivatives of it. The underlying image
was generated by Arnim Jenett and colleagues at Janelia Research Campus as part of
the FlyLight project, reported in Jenett et al. 2012, *Cell Reports* 2(4):991-1001,
<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2012.09.011>. Attribution to Jenett et al. 2012 is
required in addition to the above.

### JRC2018 bridging registrations

`bridging/JRC2018_FEMALE_20x_LPS-JFRCtemplate2010_LPS-warp.xform` and
`bridging/JRC2018_UNISEX_20xHR_8bit-JFRCtemplate2010-warp.xform` register into the
Janelia JRC2018 template spaces. The warp fields themselves are VFB's work, but the
JRC2018 templates they target are distributed by Janelia under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**:
<https://www.janelia.org/open-science/jrc-2018-brain-templates>

## History

Before August 2026 this repository carried a GPL-3.0 `LICENSE` file. GPL is a
software licence and was never an accurate description of the terms for image data.
It has been replaced rather than supplemented. Nothing in this change is intended to
narrow any permission previously granted; anyone relying on the earlier GPL grant for
their own prior use is unaffected.

In August 2026 the licence was changed again, from CC BY-SA 4.0 to **CC BY 4.0**, to match the licence named in Virtual Fly Brain's own publication (Court et al. 2023, *Front. Physiol.* 14:1076533) and to allow deposition in EMBL-EBI archives, which accept CC0 or CC BY 4.0 but not ShareAlike. This widens permissions; it does not narrow them.
